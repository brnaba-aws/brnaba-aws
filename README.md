## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🎧 [StreetLens](https://streetlensapp.com) — *Chaque lieu a une histoire.*
**GPS-triggered audio tours for the cities you visit — in your language.**

> Maps tell you where to go. Blogs tell you what to read. StreetLens lets the city speak to you.

- 🗺️ **Personalized themed walks** — Search what you're curious about (Emily in Paris, the French Revolution, Haussmann architecture) and get a tour that matches *you*
- 📍 **Single places too** — See something interesting? Tap, listen, keep walking.
- 🌍 **8 languages** — 🇬🇧 🇫🇷 🇪🇸 🇩🇪 🇮🇹 🇵🇹 🇯🇵 🇨🇳 — same street, same stories, your tongue
- 🏙️ **5 cities live** — Paris · Nantes · Honfleur · Saint-Germain-en-Laye · Concarneau
- 🔓 **Privacy by design** — no account, no ads, no tracking. Ever.

🏆 Official partner of [**Paris Je T'aime**](https://parisjetaime.com) (Paris's official tourism platform) · 🎙️ Selected for the **ElevenLabs Startup Grants** program
